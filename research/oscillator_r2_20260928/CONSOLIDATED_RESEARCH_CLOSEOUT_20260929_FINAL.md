# Final consolidated oscillator-strength research closeout

**Status:** Final evidence synthesis from independently reviewed records. Updated 2026-09-29T09:11:28+08:00. Frozen F5 reached pooled raw-f test R² 0.508624899, versus 0.485101765 for the previous equal3 and 0.455815109 for eta0: gains of +0.023523134 and +0.052809790. The test set has been reused in earlier campaigns, so this is exploratory historical-test evidence, not fresh holdout confirmation. The five-forward recipe has a 5/3 model-count cost versus equal3. Root adopted F5 as the champion for this completed round; all authorized runs are terminal.

## Metric and selection rules

The campaign objective is to improve pooled raw oscillator strength (`f`) R² while retaining every valid label; the final historical-test evaluation covers 6,686 molecules × 10 states. Development scores use the frozen training/validation/test split, and validation-selected values remain conditional on repeated use of validation. Checkpoint selection follows each study’s own reviewed protocol, not one universal loss: the five F5 component checkpoints retain their original weighted/joint validation-objective selections, including the legacy joint objective for G1/G3. No report in this synthesis removes molecule 14562 or any other case.

For historical tail analyses, q90=.0546 and q99=.2377 are **validation-derived** f cutoffs. The prospective final-test protocol uses training-only raw-f cutoffs q90=.0549 and q99=.2412. Geometry q2 bins are TRAIN-derived. See the additive [tail-threshold provenance erratum](TAIL_THRESHOLD_PROVENANCE_ERRATUM.md) and [provenance record](TAIL_THRESHOLD_PROVENANCE_ERRATUM.json). Do not describe the historical validation thresholds as TRAIN-derived.

## Final historical-test comparison and candidate decision

F5 remains the selected research champion: the exact equal-weight mean of eta0, eta01, eta1, G1, and G3, with the component checkpoints, preprocessing, and evaluator frozen before the authorized comparison. The independent review passed. The test set has appeared in earlier campaigns and was not an untouched holdout; these results are exploratory historical-test evidence, not fresh holdout confirmation.

The gain is distributed across 4,052 improving molecules and nine states; 2,634 molecules worsen. This does not identify a causal architecture change. Weaknesses remain: state 7 worsens by 0.761314 SSE; at the training-derived q99 cutoff 0.2412, the 647 brightest labels worsen by 1.199445 SSE, while the rest improve by 5.660112. The five-molecule lowest-populated q2 bin worsens by 1.518555 SSE; no test molecules fall in q2 ≤ 1e−5. No molecule was removed. The historical validation tail thresholds .0546/.2377 are validation-derived; final test slices use training-only thresholds .0549/.2412. See the [tail-threshold provenance erratum](TAIL_THRESHOLD_PROVENANCE_ERRATUM.md).

F5 needs five neural forwards versus three for old3; the 5/3 count is not a measured end-to-end latency ratio. Only G1 and G3 required new selected-checkpoint passes; three eta predictions were reused. G1/G3 checkpoints were selected by the legacy joint objective, not raw-f R². The result does not remove adaptive-selection uncertainty from the reused validation set or model-selection uncertainty from the historical test. No test-driven tuning, clipping, refit, or further candidate selection followed.

The final test protocol, frozen candidate, authorization, STARTED/terminal receipts, result hashes, and independent audit are linked in [the F5 preparation and result records](final_f5_historical_test/). The reviewed closeout supports adopting this exact F5 as the champion for the completed campaign, with the listed compute cost and remaining state/tail/concentration errors. No further experiments are planned in this completed round. A future study should confirm the recipe on a fresh, untouched holdout; this result does not establish that all architectures or reasonable approaches have been exhausted.

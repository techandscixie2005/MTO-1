## What would change priority

For the scratch pair, require intact provenance, matched fresh core/order/budget and correct raw-FP64 validation selection before comparing native versus MTO. The approved exploratory family criterion is an absolute R2 difference of at least 0.01, at least 80% positive paired molecule draws in the favored direction, and consistent connectivity-group evidence. This is a trigger to consider paired-seed confirmation, not proof or promotion; post-selection uncertainty remains descriptive. Check fixed-checkpoint training fit, final versus selected behavior, energy tradeoffs and error concentration. Concentration is a robustness diagnostic, not a hard rejection rule: valid gains on rare bright or zero-target cases legitimately improve pooled R2. A family advantage alone does not establish a useful predictor: compare absolute R2 with frozen eta0 and the existing fixed ensemble, including the newly authorized fixed scratch mean as a labeled secondary candidate. Do not prefer an architecture simply because it beats a weak matched arm. Parameter, routing and gradient-path differences remain part of this comparison.

For seed replication, prefer a predefined equal-weight seed ensemble only if its full-validation raw-f SSE improvement survives molecule/group uncertainty, reflects complementary errors across the specified seeds, and is material relative to inference cost. Report every prescribed individual and ensemble, without subset/weight search. Keep the clean legacy-selected three-seed ensemble separate from raw-f-selected new seeds and any explicitly mixed-selection ensemble using the only available legacy seed11 checkpoint. Tiny changes comparable to the frozen head's +0.00150 R2 are diagnostic, not sufficient to justify a new long study or promotion.

## If a material candidate emerges

The next training confirmation would be one independently seeded paired repeat of the promising scratch family and its matched competitor, with the same frozen split, objective, selection rule, 100-epoch budget and copied fresh common core. Choose and record the new seed before either run; do not repeat only the winner. Consistent direction and useful absolute performance across the original and second pair would strengthen evidence against an initialization accident. It would remain a small, validation-selected comparison, not an unbiased generalization estimate. A predefined seed-ensemble gain should first be judged from the already authorized seed23/37 study; do not automatically add more seeds or optimize weights.

Historical test results were reused in research. Another test query cannot provide fresh confirmation, and no automatic test reopening is justified. Independent training seeds test robustness to initialization; they do not undo validation selection or create a new untouched dataset. No new split is proposed here.

## If both scratch arms remain weak

First separate poor training fit from a train/validation gap; neither identifies a backbone ceiling. The highest-information distinct next proposal is one bounded CPU descriptor baseline before a larger backbone or coupled-state redesign: fixed connectivity/composition descriptors plus rotation-invariant geometry summaries, fitted to all ten raw-f targets using a single prespecified regularized nonlinear tree-regression recipe. Fit all preprocessing and any stopping choice using training data only; freeze the recipe before validation, with no validation-driven search. An internal training partition, if used for stopping, is not an untouched scientific holdout. Use no molecule IDs or case-specific rules.

The decisive question would be complementary error: evaluate that fixed baseline and one preregistered equal-weight blend with the chosen frozen neural comparator, using full-set raw-f R2 and the unchanged geometry strata. This is one fit with fixed readouts, not an ensemble search. Broad gains would motivate incorporating missing structural information; a negative result would reject only those descriptors and that learner. It would not reject nonlinear invariant readouts or richer backbones.

This priority follows the closed evidence: joint continuation generally regressed, frozen h residuals gained little before overfitting, and a solved linear Gram probe improved training fit without validation gain. Those findings leave nonlinear interactions and scratch optimization unresolved. Only one strict near-linear validation molecule and five adjacent low-q2 examples support the geometry concern; retain every evaluation case. Deeper messages or coupled-state/operator models remain later hypotheses, especially if systematic state-allocation errors emerge; current amplitude outliers do not establish that need.

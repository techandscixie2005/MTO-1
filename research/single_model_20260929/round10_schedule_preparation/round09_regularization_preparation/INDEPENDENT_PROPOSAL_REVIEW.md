## Interpretation and remaining preparation gates

The numerical coefficient is a single unvalidated choice. The rationale is to test parameter-norm pressure through the existing optimizer, with no architectural or target change; neither the historical record nor the coefficient's magnitude establishes that its effective update is small or optimal. A result concerns the whole post-clipping adaptive-optimizer recipe. All-parameter decay can alter learned energy offsets, radial denominators and relative feature scales; no automatic exemption, clamp, alternate coefficient or fallback is permitted. A lower norm or declining TRAIN loss does not establish a physical response mechanism, smoother spectrum or diagnosis of overfitting.

Late validation decline motivates this question but does not prove its cause, particularly because TRAIN values are trajectory averages and previous nominally identical controls vary. Both new arms must use matching diagnostic paths and workload conventions. A null result rejects this one strength/group/order/budget, not all regularization. No reused-validation bootstrap can substitute for separately authorized within-seed confirmation or independent test evidence.

Future source review, exact parameter roster, synthetic optimizer/masking/parity checks, and the proposed six discarded TRAIN updates remain unexecuted and require a new preparation decision. Numerical replay must distinguish exact RNG/order from fixed-tolerance model/Adam agreement. Strict geometry-only export, unchanged frozen schemas, registration-before-compute, immutable recovery and no-repeat/data-access guards must pass before any later publication and distinct production authority. The proposal grants none of those execution permissions.

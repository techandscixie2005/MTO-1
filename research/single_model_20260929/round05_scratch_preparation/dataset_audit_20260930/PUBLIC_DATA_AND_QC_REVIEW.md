## Verified local QC labels and unresolved provenance

The extraction manifest records `#p td=(nstates=10) b3lyp/TZVP nosymm geom=check guess=read`, Gaussian16B.01, DoRPA=T, neutral singlets and printed `Singlet-?Sym`. Coordinates are Å; excitation energy eV; length/velocity transition moments atomic units; f dimensionless. All133727 admitted records have normal termination, response convergence and complete geometry. Failed158 records were already missing those requirements. Existing masks/rounding audits—not score-based exclusions—define the valid corpus.

Only E, printed strengths and electric/velocity/magnetic moments have been audited. Full MO coefficients, AO matrices and full X/Y response amplitudes are not available in the inspected extraction. Scalar reconstruction cannot identify electronic phase, orbital convention or vector/geometry alignment. Input-frame/NoSymm provenance supports consistent axes, but the previous spotcheck did not independently prove the rotation relationship. Trace(A) is invariant to a common orthogonal frame error; directional supervision would need a stronger check. Published author metadata now supports the geometry-method description, while the TD `geom=check` route alone could not establish it.

## Scientifically justified next QC question

**Prefer a reconstruction audit on a small, fixed new-TRAIN subset before a new physical readout.** If exact basis/program settings and a suitable QC implementation become available, save complete X/Y, occupied/virtual MO coefficients, overlap S, AO dipole matrices and convergence metadata. Reconstruct length moments and printed f with explicit units and normalization before using any of these as supervision. A failed reconstruction is a provenance/convention problem to resolve, not a reason to discard hard molecules.

[PySCF's TD response implementation](https://pyscf.org/_modules/pyscf/tdscf/rhf.html) contracts length operators with X+Y and anti-Hermitian velocity operators with X−Y in its restricted convention. [Its API documentation](https://pyscf.org/pyscf_api_docs/pyscf.tdscf.html) gives restricted normalization and explicitly says the standard NTO helper ignores Y. These conventions must be reconciled with Gaussian output; an X-only orbital listing is not full-TDDFT reconstruction. Length f uses `(2/3)ω|μ|²` in atomic units. Preserve signed/complex conventions until phase-invariant observables are formed.

A future geometry-only student could learn phase-invariant transition information from such TRAIN-only QC supervision, with inference requiring no MO/X/Y labels. This would differ from the failed frozen hidden-head/right-F continuations by adding audited electronic supervision and joint learning, but improvement is a hypothesis. State sign flips leave μμ† unchanged; exactly degenerate rotations preserve summed response rather than uniquely assigning individual transition vectors. Do not treat learned state representations as normalized wavefunctions or enforce unverified state orthogonality.

[AO integral APIs](https://pyscf.org/pyscf_api_docs/pyscf.gto.html) provide overlap and position integrals from geometry plus an explicit basis. Onsite terms can avoid the nuclear-span limitation of atom charges; they do not supply the missing transition density. The previous AO feasibility note already demonstrated origin-neutrality, nonorthogonal-basis and degeneracy constraints. The existing direct PSD head spans general PSD outputs, so AO structure would be an empirical inductive bias, not a newly available output range. Any such comparison needs a matched direct head and must permit both increasing and reducing strengths.

[SchNarc's UV absorption study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07684) is evidence that geometry-based learned excited-state energies and transition moments can produce UV spectra in a small molecular setting. It does not establish transfer to QM9S's full ten-state raw-f task or guarantee0.60. A charge-only polar-vector design also retains the centrosymmetry/onsite limitations already identified here.

Available velocity strengths almost duplicate length strengths in the prior sampled-TRAIN audit. A velocity auxiliary needs a rationale beyond availability, full coverage/convention checks and matched controls; finite-basis length/velocity differences are not an exact constraint or a model noise floor. Quantum chemistry should first improve supervision/provenance. Repeating F/decorrelation, raw-f continuation or hidden-head experiments with a new physical name is not justified.

# Next decision after Round03

Analysis only, written while the fixed 33-epoch source runs. No new calibration results, model inference, GPU polling, implementation or fitting informed this note. Rank only two directions; neither is authorized by this document.

## What the completed evidence establishes

- Round01 control/F/raw-M-decorrelation/both all selected the starting epoch 33 checkpoint. The interventions were active. At epoch 20 the control's pooled SSE rose 6.1206, while false-bright-bin SSE rose 10.0074; gains elsewhere partly offset that increase. This is not uniform deterioration. Global S7/S8 summed-error SSE also increased, so pure strength redistribution is not a sufficient explanation.
- Round02 froze every original parameter/buffer. F still moved its input by about 5–6%, online TRAIN errors fell, and no batch clipped gradients. Selected native gains were only 0.000114/0.000093; epoch 20 R² deteriorated to 0.403126/0.402158. Direct raw-f supervision did not beat the matched trace arm. An inert adapter, clipping, or moving the original backbone cannot explain the entire result.
- The full baseline's TRAIN affine moments favor approximately 1.00359*f+0.0000905, whereas the exposed historical validation map is 0.85118*f+0.003725. This supports an error-distribution difference. It does not prove that this difference caused the readout failures or that a richer function would transfer successfully.
- All campaign F/decorrelation fits started from the already validation-selected seed11 epoch 33 model or froze it. Their null results concern adaptation around that solution. They do not test jointly learning F and the original representation from random initialization.

## 1. Small observable readout trained on genuine held-out predictions

**Priority if Round03 passes its existing gate.** Prepare a matched pair of identical small heads: one trained from the clean source's predictions on its reserved 20%, the other from the full baseline's predictions on those SAME molecules/labels. Both deploy with the unchanged full baseline in one complete checkpoint. A gate pass authorizes preparation only, not fitting or a new-best claim.

Use predicted E in eV and native f as the first candidate inputs. These have the same observable definitions in independently trained models. Hidden h/M channels can rotate, permute or change scale without an agreed cross-model coordinate system; equal tensor shapes do not justify transferring a head between them. Observable inputs avoid that alignment problem, although their distributions can still differ because the auxiliary source uses 80% of TRAIN and has different errors. State identity/order and near-degeneracy remain benchmark conventions, not recovered electronic gauges.

The smallest next question is nonlinear correction versus the already frozen affine maps. Start with a shared state-local residual g(E_k,f_k), initialized to preserve the native prediction with a nonzero useful output-layer gradient. Keep every backbone parameter/buffer fixed and energy unchanged; fit all valid printed-f labels with the same raw-f MSE normalization, capacity, initialization, sample order, optimizer and prespecified budget in both source arms. Fix feature scaling without outer labels. The nonnegativity rule must be identical. Do not add output caps, robust-loss changes, selective sampling or label exclusions to this comparison. Final affine/nonlinear f may differ from E*trace(A); state that explicitly.

Use the native anchor, both Round03 affine maps and the exposed historical affine as controls. The two matched nonlinear arms isolate the proposed prediction-source procedure; comparison with the affine maps tests whether added flexibility is useful. Neither contrast alone identifies a cross-state interaction. If that becomes a separate question, add a matched local-versus-context head comparison with the source fixed. Inputs such as adjacent predicted gaps and total predicted strength are plausible context features, but introducing them immediately would combine two questions.

Report pooled/per-state errors, true-bright tails, all four true/predicted-bright bins and error concentration. SSE's sensitivity to rare large predictions is part of the target, not a reason to omit those rows. Genuine independently initialized base seeds are required before a broader gain claim; never average their predictions. If Round03 fails, do not proceed to a nonlinear version solely on the same transfer rationale. First interpret the fixed source's quality and its training-size mismatch; failure of this one source is not a theorem against held-out calibration.

## 2. A matched F study from random initialization

**A genuinely untested question in the audited records, with a larger compute cost.** Joint learning could make the original representation use the pre-CG transformation differently than adding it after selection. This is a hypothesis, not an expected improvement or a causal account of the null continuations. The original eta0 epoch 33 optimizer state is unavailable, which further limits interpretations of reset-optimizer continuation, but does not explain away frozen-F failure.

Historical scratch evidence is cautionary, not a clean rejection: seed23/37 selected native R² were about 0.368709/0.394455 versus seed11's0.405294, with substantial late TRAIN improvement and validation deterioration. Their selected epochs 53/44 and 100-epoch caps differ from seed11's236-epoch stopping history; these are not a matched-budget seed-variance estimate. Chan64 G1 reached 0.405255 but also used eta1; G3 reached 0.368101 with a p-only head and E²-weighted trace. Neither is a width-only comparison. The 100-epoch scaled-objective scratch MTO/direct-f studies reached 0.373493/0.343782 and did not test this right-F architecture.

If root chooses this direction, the smallest first stage is ONE original-MTO/control versus identity-F pair from a shared random seed, rather than another continuation. A prespecified 100-epoch stage covers the historical seed23/37 selection times; a shorter stage must be called preliminary. Use the original full-TRAIN statistics, architecture, objective and optimizer, identical initial base tensors and data order, reset RNG after differing constructors, and validation-only pooled native-f selection. For an architecture contrast, predeclare a common realized learning-rate schedule. An identical adaptive scheduler rule can produce different realized schedules and would instead test that combined training procedure. Compare the contemporaneous control as well as historical incumbents. F must preserve all outputs at initialization and have live gradients. Freeze the schedule/budget before fitting; no width/loss/dropout/weight-decay changes belong inside this comparison.

A two-arm result cannot support decorrelation or interaction claims. Those require the original four-arm control/F/decorrelation/both factorial from the same initialization, with the existing weak normalized raw-state penalty and no physical-wavefunction orthogonality claim. Advance only a prespecified promising contrast to genuine seed confirmation. No prediction averaging or historical-test selection is allowed. This direction does not logically require a Round03 pass, but spending its budget requires a separate root decision after Round03; it is not an automatic response to failure.

## Do not spend the next budget on these repeats

Do not extend unchanged full-model or frozen-F continuations, repeat the historical frozen h-only raw-f head, or assume direct-f/width changes are untried. History found no documented controlled dropout/weight-decay sweep in the reviewed evidence, but this is not a repository-wide absence claim. One simple regularization control could later test a competing explanation; combining it with F would lose attribution and a broad sweep is unsupported.

Keep physical response proposals below these two questions. Existing coherent within-state tensor interference is already present. Pure state rotation cannot attenuate total strength, and its latent-factor gauges remain unresolved. A nuclear point-charge covariance cannot represent out-of-plane dipoles of planar geometries. Geometry plus a fixed basis can supply AO overlap/dipole integrals without MO/X/Y; their conventions remain unvalidated and the integral library is absent. Missing MO/X/Y still prevents verified transition-density/NTO supervision. The existing direct PSD head already spans PSD outputs, so AO structure is a deferred empirical prior, not a demonstrated missing output range or cure for false-bright errors.

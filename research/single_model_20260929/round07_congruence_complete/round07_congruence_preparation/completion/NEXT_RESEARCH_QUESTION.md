# Next question: what limits the original MTO representation and training recipe?

Recommendation: complete a read-only bottleneck audit before another fit. Repeated small readout changes have not surpassed the retained original-model reference. The next question should be whether state-specific information is lost before the PSD readout, or whether the original representation's usable accuracy is limited chiefly by the current optimization/generalization recipe. These are competing hypotheses, not diagnoses established by this campaign.

Round07 makes the distinction useful: gates were live, tensor changes were substantial, and logged q exceeded1/3, yet tensor did not beat scalar or the retained reference. TRAIN loss continued to fall while validation raw-f performance deteriorated, especially through false-bright predictions. Earlier fresh F/decor and raw-f objective comparisons also failed their gates. Same declared seed/init/order control outcomes varied from.447169 to.404798 and.409104, with different documented execution graphs/workloads. Neither inactive correction nor a proven lack of model capacity is an adequate summary. The errors do not establish incorrect labels or a QC mechanism.

## One bounded audit, then one falsifiable proposal

Use source and existing aggregate records only. Trace the actual information path from the unchanged DetaNet core through atom/molecule pooling, ten-state routing/query features, raw M states and ground–excited CG coupling to scalar/tensor channels and energy/strength outputs. Identify exact dimensional bottlenecks, state sharing, residual paths and what full-training gradients can change. Compare these facts with the already saved TRAIN/validation/state/tail trajectories and clipping records. Do not infer a representational defect merely because a channel is narrow.

Build a small source-backed ledger of prior width/depth, normalization, regularization and LR-schedule trials, stating which factors co-changed. The audited core is128 features, three interaction blocks, l<=3,32 Bessel functions, eight attention heads and5Å radius, with dropout0. Existing channel64 G1 used eta1/Q supervision; G3 also changed the tensor construction and E-squared weighting. Their.405255/.368101 outcomes do not isolate width. The historical100-epoch.373493 study changed to a direct-f head, a raw-f objective, energy initialization and a stepped LR schedule; it does not isolate schedule on the current original PSD LE+Ls model. The original eta0 run used plateau/early-stop rules and selected epoch33 on the old split. Absence of a clean depth or regularization comparison in this audited ledger is not a claim of repository-wide absence.

The audit should end with one discriminating experiment proposal: either an unchanged original model with one prespecified optimization/regularization change and a contemporary control, if the history shows that factor is genuinely untested; or one upstream representation change tied to an exact information-loss argument, retaining the original readout/objective and a matched control. Do not combine those factors, sweep choices or default to another scalar/tensor correction. Freeze the baseline execution/diagnostic path and quantify repeat-control uncertainty before assigning a small gain to architecture. A larger or deeper backbone is not recommended merely from the.60 target.

A useful falsifiable claim would be: a specific, documented upstream bottleneck prevents better per-state generalization even when the original model continues fitting TRAIN; removing only that bottleneck improves pooled validation raw-f beyond both a contemporary unchanged control and the retained reference, without unacceptable state/tail/energy regressions. Source/history evidence must specify the bottleneck before a protocol can test this claim. If the audit instead favors schedule or regularization, state and test that narrower claim first. No outcome is presumed.
